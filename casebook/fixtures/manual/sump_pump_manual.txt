SP-200 Submersible Sump Pump -- Operation and Maintenance Manual (quorum test fixture, written for this project)

1. Instruments
The pump controller reports four signals. The high-water float switch closes when the water in the pit is above the
high-water mark. The motor current relay closes when the motor is drawing running current. The discharge flow switch
closes when water is moving in the discharge pipe. The thermal switch in the motor winding opens when the winding is
overheated.

2. Normal operation
When the high-water float switch is closed, the pump should be running. When the float switch is open and no other
signal is present, the pump is idle and no action is needed.

3. Faults
If the high-water float switch is closed and the motor current relay is not closed, the pump has failed to start and
the pit may overflow. A pump that has failed to start must be reported to maintenance immediately.
If the motor current relay is closed and the discharge flow switch is not closed, the pump is running dry or the
discharge is blocked. Running dry destroys the shaft seal within minutes, so the pump must be stopped.
If the thermal switch has opened, the motor is overheated and the pump must be stopped.

4. Exceptions
During the weekly test the operator runs the pump with the pit empty for up to one minute. While the weekly test is in
progress, running with no discharge flow is expected and is not a fault.

5. Instrument checks
Water cannot move in the discharge pipe unless the motor is drawing current. If the discharge flow switch is closed
while the motor current relay is open, one of the two instruments is faulty and both must be inspected.

6. Priorities
Stopping the pump takes priority over reporting to maintenance, and reporting to maintenance takes priority over
normal running.
