435 rows: 267 democrat, 168 republican. 16 votes per row, each y, n, or ? (392 ? in total).
'?' does not mean "unknown" in the usual sense: the dataset's documentation says it covers paired against, announced
against, voted present, and did not vote. It is encoded here as the ABSENCE of both the y and the n feature, which is
the honest reading -- it asserts nothing -- and it is exactly what AA-CBR's "irrelevant case" rule needs.
